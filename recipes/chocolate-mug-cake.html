Chocolate Mug Cake

Description

This chocolate mug cake is made in the microwave for a fudgy, chocolaty treat that is truly decadent. It's a great recipe when you need a delicious dessert that's ready in less than 10 minutes! Add a few chocolate chips to make it extra rich and gooey.

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup white sugar
  • 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/8 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 3 tablespoons milk
  • 2 tablespoons canola oil
  • 1 tablespoon water
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Whisk flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt together in a large microwave-safe mug; stir in milk, canola oil, water, and vanilla extract.
  3. Cook in the microwave until cake is done in the middle, about 1 minute 45 seconds.
  4. Enjoy!

Cook's Note

For a lighter version, use 1 ½ tablespoons plain unsweetened applesauce instead of canola oil, and use 3 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon water instead of milk.
